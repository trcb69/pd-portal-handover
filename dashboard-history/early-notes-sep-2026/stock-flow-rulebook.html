2 · When Zoho moves stock

A sales order reserves stock. A shipment takes it away.

Zoho keeps two stock figures. Physical stock goes up on a purchase receive and down on a shipment. Accounting stock goes up on a bill and down on an invoice. Each organisation chooses which one its screens show (Settings → Preferences → Items). Our stock system compares shelf counts with physical stock, because that is what a count can check.

5 · Procedure

Abdullah: SH stock (Rizwan's department)

  1. Reorder from buffer limits, weekly

    Every item's buffer limit is its reorder level in Zoho SH. Each week, take the list of items at or below it, set each quantity to buffer limit − stock on hand − quantity already on open POs, and send it to Rizwan. Nazmi raises the PO in Zoho; the COO approves it in Zoho.

    Today the list is a hand-built Excel, and only 116 of 12,006 SH items have a reorder level. With the levels in Zoho, our stock system builds the list. recommended

  2. Record goods-in the same day

    Count the delivery against the PO. Record the purchase receive in Zoho on the day the goods arrive, at the location they physically went to. Record short or damaged deliveries on the receive, not later as an adjustment.

    A late receive makes stock look short for days; at SPS the median delay is 2 days.

  3. Receive SmartPack goods into SH stock

    When SPS ships to SH (SPS shipment + ITN invoice), record the receipt into SH stock the same day, quoting the SPS invoice number. Then arrange the order for processing: Team 03 sales order and picklist.

    Every SPS invoice to SH must have a matching SH receipt. Our stock system lists unmatched ones daily. recommended owner: Abdullah

  4. Stock leaves only against a picklist

    Items leave SH stock only through a sales order → picklist → package → shipment (or an IBR for branches). There is no "take now, record later".

    Negative stock is the signal that something left without a document.

  5. Count daily, correct with approval

    Count the day's list (about 20 items the system flags) at each location. For a difference, look for the missing document first. Only then adjust, with a reason. Differences above the value limit need Rizwan's approval.

    Small daily counts replace the big monthly bursts. The value limit is for Rizwan and the COO to set. recommended

6 · Procedure

Ilham: SPS stores

  1. Goods-in to the Raw Material Warehouse, same day

    Count against the PO. Record the purchase receive into Raw Material Warehouse (not WIP) on the day of arrival. Note shortages or damage on the receive.

    Today raw material is received straight into WIP, so the raw-material store has no real stock figure. recommended owner: Ilham

  2. Issue material only with a transfer order

    Every issue to the floor is a Zoho transfer order Raw Material → WIP, with the job number as the reference, made the same day. If a paper slip stays for signatures, its number goes on the transfer order. Material returned from the floor is a transfer back.

    This one step removes the negative WIP stock (23 items) and makes "is the raw material available?" answerable.

  3. Production records (coordinators)

    Coordinators keep recording bundles on the day. Every damage adjustment carries the reason, the job number and a short description. None blank.

    373 adjustments in 12 months have no job number, so their loss can't be traced to a job or division.

  4. Finished goods into the Finished Goods Warehouse

    When goods leave the floor, transfer WIP → Finished Goods, or bundle straight into Finished Goods. Pack and ship only from Finished Goods.

    Today 721 finished items hold stock in WIP and 4 in Finished Goods, so "ready to ship" can't be seen.

  5. Dispatch to SH

    Package and shipment from Finished Goods on the day goods leave. Darshani raises the ITN invoice to SH. The SH side records the receipt (Abdullah).

    The shipment date must be the day the goods left, not the day the paperwork was done.

  6. Count daily, correct with approval

    Count the system's daily list in all three warehouses. Look for the missing document first; adjust with a reason only after. Differences above the value limit need the Production GM's approval.

    In 215 of 364 stock counts, more stock was found than Zoho showed, which points to unrecorded receipts and returns. recommended approver

8 · Still to confirm

Recommended, not yet confirmed

Where the current practice wasn't known, this page recommends what works best for the software. Confirm or correct these:

  • Who raises SPS purchase orders, and who approves them (recommended: Ilham lists, Production GM checks, purchasing raises, approval in Zoho).
  • That Ilham records SPS goods-in, and that Abdullah records SPS goods arriving into SH stock.
  • That material is issued to production on paper today (the data suggests so: 47 transfer orders in 13 months).
  • The value limit above which a stock correction needs approval, and the approvers (recommended: Rizwan for SH, Production GM for SPS).
  • Whether SH receives SPS goods by purchase receive or by another document.
  • SH Zoho access for all 10 locations, so SH checks cover all its stock.
