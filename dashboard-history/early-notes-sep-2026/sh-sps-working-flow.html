Side A · the factory

SmartPack: how a customised job runs today

SmartPack makes the printed and customised items. 99.9% of what it invoices goes to SH; the real end customer is written on the SPS order in the field “Order Received from”.

Order arrivesIn the one WhatsApp order group, or by phone.
Triage picks itThe team decides what goes to production.
Job is bornThe division manager writes it in the monthly Excel sheet.
File + Zoho orderA coordinator makes the paper file and the SPS sales order, same day.
ProductionFile carried by hand. Job card, processing order, inline inspection.
File returnsCoordinator records assemblies, picklists and stock adjustments in Zoho.
Ilham shipsStores writes the shipment reference.
Darshani invoicesInvoice (ITN) to SH. SH now owns the goods.
  • The coordinator pairs: Ranga + Nirmani, Ishan + Umanda, Mubeena with Nimesha and Madushi. Offset jobs go to either pair, chosen per job.
  • Ex-stock is different: paper straws and rewinding start inside Ishan's own area, often made before the file exists.
  • Where it hurts: no start time is recorded anywhere; the paper file's location is the only status; customised jobs are on time only about 57% of the time; in September 31% of screen-printing jobs were blocked because the raw material was not there.
  • Stock in SPS's own Zoho: almost everything sits in the work-in-progress warehouse. 721 finished items are in WIP against 4 in the finished-goods store, 23 items show negative stock, and only 47 transfer orders were made in 13 months, so issuing to the floor is done on paper.
