Data to fix

Items whose cost doesn't match how they're sold

These items show an impossible margin (a loss of more than 100%, or over 97% profit), almost always because they're bought in one unit and sold in another (kg vs g, box vs piece). They're left out of the profit figures above. Fixing the units in Zoho makes the margins trustworthy.
