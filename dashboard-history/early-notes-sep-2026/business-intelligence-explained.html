What is business intelligence, and are Zoho's reports it?

A two-minute read. Short answer: Zoho Inventory's reports are reports, not business intelligence. Zoho does sell a BI tool (Zoho Analytics), but even that can only see what is typed into Zoho, and most of our process isn't.

Business intelligence, in one sentence

Business intelligence turns the records of all the business's systems into answers to management questions — why something happened, where it's going and what to do — kept consistent and up to date, so decisions don't wait for someone to build a spreadsheet.

A report tells you what happened inside one system. BI tells you why, across systems, and what to do next.

Three different things

Report

Zoho Inventory reports

"What did we sell last month, by item?"

  • Pre-built lists and totals: sales by item, by customer, purchases, stock summary.
  • One Zoho organisation at a time: SH or SmartPack.
  • Only what was typed into Zoho Inventory.
BI tool

e.g. Zoho Analytics, Power BI

"Why did Specialities margin drop, and which customers caused it?"

  • Joins data from several sources, with trends, drill-down and alerts.
  • Needs someone to model the data and define the measures.
  • Still blind to anything that lives on paper or WhatsApp.
What we're building

Command Centre + production dashboard

"Where is job 6700, what's blocking it, and who owns the fix?"

  • BI across both companies, plus the steps Zoho never sees: order arrival, file location, blockers, output.
  • Answers questions and drives action: work lists, alerts, owners.
  • Read-only on Zoho; built for our process.

Two examples from our own data

Zoho report: "SH sold LKR 2.17 billion in the last 12 months."
BI: "Up 11%; Specialities is 46% of it; LKR 225M is overdue from customers, listed by account and by how late."
Zoho report: "Pizza-box order dated 3 Aug; shipment dated in December."
BI: "Production took 7 hours, on 11 Aug. The other four months were waiting and paperwork, and 31% of Screen Printing jobs wait on raw material. The bottleneck is readiness, not machines."

So why not just buy Zoho Analytics?

  • It's a good tool for charts on Zoho data, and it could sit on top of what we build.
  • But BI is only as good as the data under it. Our biggest gaps (when an order arrived, where the job file is, why a job is blocked, what was produced today) aren't in Zoho at all. They're on paper and in WhatsApp.
  • That's why the paperless job file comes first: it creates the data. The dashboard then turns it into answers, at no licence cost, on our own server.

Zoho Analytics is a paid add-on; we haven't priced or trialled it. Figures above come from both companies' Zoho data to 15 Sep 2026.
