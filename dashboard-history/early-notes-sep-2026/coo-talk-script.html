What I found 8 MIN · PICK 2–3

  • Time isn't lost on machines. A Screen Printing job was made in 7 hours, but took about 4 months from order to shipment. 31% of Screen Printing jobs are blocked waiting for raw material.
  • Cash: customers owe SH LKR 324M, 225M of it overdue. 2,928 orders are open over 60 days (≈ LKR 230M).
  • Order value is unreliable: 41% of orders are invoiced for a different amount; 29% are keyed with no price.
  • Returns: credit notes were LKR 241M, and 197M is typed "Other", so the reason is unknown.
  • Surprise: a lot of SH stock sits at the SmartPack factory in SH's books; Galle and Kandy are supplied from it.

What's built 5 MIN

"I learned the full process of both companies, WhatsApp order to invoice, and pulled every record from both Zoho accounts: over 400,000. There's a Command Centre demo for you, the CEO and both GMs. The dashboard foundation is built and tested (136 automated checks, plus separate code, security and database reviews), and it's ready to go live privately this week."

If the COO asks… ANSWERS

  • When is it usable? The foundation goes live this week. The next release, the order portal and the digital job file, is when people see orders move.
  • Will staff resist? The pilot starts with IT keying the data. Managers get four taps only: receive, output, blocker, send back.
  • Cost? Our own server. Only the floor devices and people's time during the pilot.
  • Why not just Zoho? Zoho sees the order, then the shipment. The four months in between happen on paper.

The decision 8 MIN · THE HEART

"Mr. Samitha's brief was to cut documentation time and keep it accurate. That only works if the coordinator's job file goes paperless: created once, and every step after is a tap, timed automatically."

"Keep the paper file, and people fill in the paper, the Excel sheet and the dashboard. That's more documentation, the opposite of the brief. Only 4 of 17 features fully work."

"My suggestion: a two-week pilot in Screen Printing, with IT keying at first so nobody does double work. What do you think?"

Then stop and listen. The answer here matters more than anything else in the meeting.
