Question 1

What is an IBR?

IBR = Inter-Branch Requisition. It is a branch asking head office for stock. Galle writes “send me these items”, and head office moves the stock from the factory store to the Galle store.

The short version

  • It is not a sale. No customer, no price, no invoice. Only stock moving between our own warehouses.
  • In Zoho it is a Transfer Order, numbered like TOS-260973-GALLE, with the reason Interbranch.
  • The IBR number is the branch's own request number, typed into the transfer order's description: IBR1070SOU. SOU = South, that is Galle.
  • Only after the transfer arrives can Galle sell that stock to its own customers, as a normal sales order.

Question 2

Retail sales, and what “retail chain” means here

In Zoho, Retail is a tick on the sales order called Order Pick Type. It means a small over-the-counter sale, not a supermarket chain.

Type of saleOrdersAverage valueWho it is
Retail15,731LKR 15,613Counter and cash sales: “Cash Customer”, staff sales, small repeat buyers

Key Customer8,003LKR 60,668The actual chains: Spar 811 orders, Jaykay (Keells) 562
  • Retail happens at two places only: Head Office 13,813 orders and Kandy 1,917. Galle has one.
  • The supermarket chains are not tagged “Retail”. Spar and Jaykay orders are tagged Key Customer, and they go out on the Katunayake and Kollupitiya delivery routes.
  • A counter sale still becomes a full Zoho order. Same four documents as any other sale — nothing is skipped.
  • The give-away is the delivery route GUARD ROOM: the customer collects at the gate rather than being delivered to.
Question 3

Consumption: stock we use ourselves

When a warehouse uses stock instead of selling it, it is booked as a sale to a pretend customer named after the warehouse.

How to recognise it

  • Customer name ends in “(Consumption)”: Kelaniya Warehouse (Consumption) 656 orders, Ranaviru 128, Central 5, South 4.
  • Order number starts INTSO and the invoice type is Internal Transfers (776 of 793 orders).
  • The delivery route is INTERNAL — nothing leaves the premises.
  • Most carry no money: 494 of 793 orders have a zero value. The rest total LKR 2.2 M.

Why it exists: it is the only way to take the stock out of Zoho's count. Otherwise Zoho keeps showing goods that were already used up.

Question 4 · the one you asked

Do IBR and consumption behave like a normal order?

Consumption: yes, identical

It runs through exactly the same four Zoho steps as a customer sale — sales order, package, shipment, invoice. Only the customer, the invoice type and the route are different.

IBR: no, it is a different document

An IBR never becomes a sales order. It is a transfer order, with no customer, no package, no shipment and no invoice. The sale happens later and separately, when the branch sells that stock on.

Question 7

What to watch out for

1

IBR numbers live in free text

They sit in the transfer order's description, typed by hand. 277 transfers carry two or more IBR numbers at once, and one is typed “IBRNO1084SOU”. Nothing can match an IBR to what was actually sent, so no one can prove a branch got what it asked for.

2

Only Galle uses IBR numbers

Kandy's 684 transfers carry no request number at all. Two branches, two habits.

3

Picking cannot be tied to an order

Zoho holds 76,855 picklists, but the list carries no order number and no customer — both fields are empty on every single one. So “who picked this order and when” cannot be answered from the data today.

4

Internal invoices sit in the debtors list

The consumption warehouses have 802 invoices worth LKR 5.6 M, and 210 of them show as overdue, LKR 1.96 M outstanding. We are chasing ourselves for money, and it inflates the receivables figure.

5

The order value is not the real money — this is the big one

Of 47,389 completed orders that carry a price, 19,430 (41%) were invoiced for a different amount: 12,699 for less, 6,731 for more. Only 115 of them are flagged “partly invoiced”, so the gap is invisible in Zoho. Anyone reporting on order value gets a different answer from anyone reporting on invoices.

6

29% of orders are keyed with no price at all

20,638 orders carry a zero value, and only 5,417 of those are samples. 14,028 of them were later invoiced for real money. The price is decided at invoicing, not at ordering, so the order is not a promise of anything.

7

Documents dated before the goods move

Order NOTTOBEUSED X0008 (Kandy, 31 Mar) was invoiced on 31 March but packed and shipped on 30 April, a month later. Seventeen orders are numbered “NOTTOBEUSED”, and one order is dated in the year 1831.

8

The tags are not applied consistently

330 of the 793 consumption orders are tagged “Retail”, and 56 chain orders (Spar, Jaykay, Keells) are tagged “Retail” instead of “Key Customer”. Any report split by pick type is therefore approximate.

9

“Internal” consumption sometimes leaves by the gate

41 consumption orders were shipped on the GUARD ROOM route rather than INTERNAL, so someone collected them. Whether that is stock used internally or handed out is not recorded.

10

One transfer, one arrival — not checked

Of the IBR transfers, 5 are still “in transit” and 1 only “approved”. Nothing alerts anyone when stock stays in transit.

Source: Zoho Inventory, Standard Holdings organisation, read-only pull of 19 Sep 2026. Sales orders, transfer orders, packages, shipments and invoices, in full.

Invoices were matched to orders by customer, date and amount; where the amounts differ, that is called out rather than guessed.
