1 · The two companies

Goods and money go both ways

SmartPack isn't just SH's supplier. SH owns stock inside the factory, sells it materials, buys back the finished goods, and sells stock to SmartPack's own retail accounts.

5 · Where it breaks

Ten problems the records show

The order's proof arrives last

The customer's PO or WhatsApp message is attached to the invoice (6,400 files in 12 months) more often than to the sales order (3,994). Nobody can check an order against what the customer asked for until it has shipped.

Fix: the order portal records the request, with its file and time, before the sales order exists.

Approvals happen in WhatsApp, on paper and by email

New items and prices are approved in a WhatsApp group; stock adjustments on signed paper ("pending approval"); credit notes by email. Zoho gets a picture, not a decision with a name and a time.

Fix: approval steps in our software (credit hold, item and price approval, adjustment approval), each recording who and when.

Orders are never closed

2,928 SH sales orders are open more than 60 days (LKR 230M); 302 are over a year old. SmartPack has 1,626 open over 60 days.

Fix: a daily "old open orders" list to each order owner; close or cancel.

Stock is reserved that doesn't exist

869 SH items and 686 SmartPack items have more stock reserved by open orders than on hand; 165 SH items show negative stock.

Fix: clearing old orders releases the reservations; the stock system flags negatives daily.

The factory's warehouses aren't used

SmartPack has raw-material, WIP and finished-goods warehouses but works in WIP: 47 transfer orders in 13 months, 23 items negative in WIP, 721 finished items sitting in WIP.

Fix: issue by transfer order, finish into Finished Goods (stock procedure page).

Units don't match, so margins lie

Items bought in one unit and sold in another show impossible margins (e.g. −23,414% on a paste). About 4.5% of SH sales (LKR 98M) has no reliable cost.

Fix: correct the units in Zoho; the dashboard lists them.

Samples and loans have no recorded value

2,436 sample notes in 12 months total LKR 0.5M (about LKR 200 each); 419 inventory loans were recorded at zero on the order. What the samples cost is invisible.

Fix: value samples at cost; track loans until returned or invoiced.

Goods-in is recorded late

SmartPack records purchase receives a median 2 days after arrival (up to 14), so stock looks short while it's on the shelf.

Fix: same-day receiving, checked daily.

Money crosses between companies informally

A customer payment into SmartPack's bank account was filed as an SH customer payment. One example so far; it needs checking with finance before it's called a pattern.

Fix: confirm the rule with finance; flag payments whose bank account doesn't match the company.

The factory's evidence is paper-only

No goods receipts, POs or job records carry files in SmartPack's Zoho; the job file travels desk to desk, and "where is the file?" is the status.

Fix: the production dashboard (file location, stages, output, taps on the day).
