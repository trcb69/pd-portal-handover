Today vs Tomorrow: SH and SmartPack
Standard Holdings + SmartPack · operations · 19 Sep 2026

How orders, production and stock run today, and how they will run

The same three views twice: the operation, the system used at each step, and where the data goes. Today, one order is typed again at up to five points between WhatsApp, Excel, paper and two Zoho organisations. In the proposed system, each fact is entered once, where it happens, and our software reads Zoho for the rest.
